# User Guide

DISPO OPS tracks every property in the dispositions pipeline as a checklist (the SOP for its exit strategy). Every change shows for the whole team straight away.

## Signing In

Click **Sign In With Google** and use your @totalchb.com account. Other accounts are refused.

## The Top Of Every Screen

- **Light / Dark** switches the look; your choice is remembered on that device.
- **Active** shows who is online now, on phone or desktop.
- The two tabs: **Master Dashboard** and **Active Deal Tracker**. **SOP Reference** is in the menu under your photo. On a phone the two tabs are in the bar at the bottom, with **Chat**, and SOP Reference is in the photo menu too.
- The bell opens your **Notifications**; your photo opens **Notification Settings**, **Team**, **Help** and **Sign Out**.
- Under the title, the badge says **Live** when everything is saved, **Saving…** while a change is on its way, and **Not Saved · Retry** if a change could not be saved (click it to try again). The clock is Eastern time.

## Master Dashboard

- Deals are **Open** by default. Use **Closed** and **Cancelled** to see finished or dropped deals.
- Filter by exit strategy and sort by Recently Edited, Oldest Edited, Newest Added, Oldest Added or **Due Soonest**.
- Each row shows the address, strategy, when it started, how long since the last edit, the **Next Agent**, the **Next Step**, the due date and the % complete.
- Click a row to open the deal. Click the **Next Step** to jump straight to that task.
- **Deploy New Asset Tracker** starts a new deal: type the address, pick the exit strategy and, for Fix & Flip, optionally attach a SOW CSV (Task, Cost, Notes): each row becomes a subtask of Create SOW.

## The Deal Tracker

- **Back To Master Dashboard** returns to the list. The arrows go to the **Previous** and **Next** deal in the order and filter the dashboard is showing (for example "3 of 18").
- The header shows the address, strategy, status, when it was created and last updated, and the **Total Progress** with the next step.
- **Set Due Date** gives the whole deal a due date (for example the target closing). **Next Due** shows the earliest open task or subtask due date; click it to jump there. Anything overdue turns red.
- **Notes**, **Files & Links** and **Activity** in the header gather everything on the deal: every note and draft, every link and file, and every change with who made it and when, each tagged with its task.
- **All Agents** shows one person's tasks only.

## Working The Checklist

The checklist is grouped by phase, then by group (for example Underwriting). A group shows its % and **Complete** or **Pending**; finished groups fold away by themselves. Click a group to open or close it.

- **Tick the box** on a task to mark it done. The **+%** pill is how much the task counts toward the deal's progress.
- Click the **owner** to give the task to someone else. People are grouped by department, as in the Universal Team Hub.
- Some steps ask you to **Select Path For This Step** first (for example Local or Non-Local Contractor): the path sets the title and the owner. **Edit Path** lets you choose again.
- **Add Subtask** adds your own item under a task. Each subtask is **Not Started**, **In Progress** or **Done**; when every subtask is Done, the task is done. The pencil edits a subtask; **Remove** asks you to type Confirm.
- Message drafts and links go in **Details**: write them as a **Note**, add links under **Files**. Drafts and links saved before are still listed there, with **Copy** and **Open**, and can be removed.
- The small icons on every task and subtask: **Notes** (who wrote each and when; on a task, switch on **Include Subtask Notes** to see its subtasks' notes too), **Files & Links** (Upload Files, up to 25 MB each, or Add Link), the **calendar** (due date and time, Eastern, 9:00 AM unless you change it) and **Activity**.

## Inject Custom Task

Adds a task that only this deal needs. Choose the phase, an existing group or a new one (and where it goes), the title, the owner and, if it should count toward progress, a weight. A weight has to be taken from other tasks so the deal still totals 100%: add the tasks to borrow from until the badge says **100% Balanced**. Tick **Split this task's weight equally across its subtasks** to have each of its subtasks count for an equal share of its weight (as a CSV SOW task does). Deleting a custom task gives its weight back.

## Closing Or Cancelling A Deal

Use **Status** in the deal header: **Mark As Closed** or **Mark As Cancelled**. The deal moves to that view on the dashboard, its checklist becomes read-only (chat stays open) and it stops sending alerts. **Reopen** brings it back to Open.

## Chat

The round button (or **Chat** on a phone) opens the discussions. Without a deal open you see every deal's chat with its unread count. In a deal's chat you can pin messages, search, see **Pinned Messages**, and open **Links & Attachments** (every link and draft on the deal, with Copy). "Read by" shows who has seen a message.

## Notifications

- The bell lists your alerts; click one to open the deal (or the chat message). Select several to mark them read or delete them.
- **Notification Settings**: **Enable Push On This Device** for phone and desktop alerts, choose which alerts you get, and how often **Overdue Reminders** repeat. Overdue Reminders cover tasks waiting on you and due dates that have passed: you get one when a due date passes, then one on your interval until it is done.
- **Wipe Devices** stops push on devices you no longer use; **Defaults** restores the standard settings for your role.

## If Something Looks Wrong

If the badge says **Not Saved**, click it to try again. After an update, reload the page with **Ctrl+Shift+R** (Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).
